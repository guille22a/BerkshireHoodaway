Who can execute allocation changes in the 24-hour Timelock?

Only the project owner / deployer. Both proposeAllocations() and executeAllocations() are protected by the onlyOwner modifier. Nobody else can propose or execute allocation changes. The 24-hour timelock exists so the community gets 24 hours of advance on-chain visibility before the owner finalizes an update.

Why is there no MEV sandwich risk when triggering the buy?

The frontend interface queries current DEX quotes and calculates strict 5% max slippage bounds (amountOutMin) before broadcasting the transaction. If an MEV bot attempts to front-run or sandwich the swap beyond 5%, the transaction automatically reverts, protecting 100% of the funds.

Can the dev or owner withdraw tokens from the vault?

No. There are literally no withdrawal functions in the contract. Even the owner cannot extract tokens or unstake LP tokens. The code is immutable.
